# Broadway Tonight — Privacy Policy (DRAFT — for lawyer review, not legal advice)
_Last updated: [DATE]_
**What we collect**
- **Ratings:** a 1–5 star rating of a *bar*, tagged to the set/band playing at that time. No written reviews or free text.
- **Vibe tags:** optional pick (e.g. loud, chill, packed, dancing, tipsy) attached to a rating.
- **Check-ins:** optional "I'm here" flag (later: approximate location to confirm you're on Broadway; never stored as continuous tracking).
- **Stage photos (optional):** shown publicly for **24 hours only**, then archived (kept in storage, hidden from public). "Photos stay up 24 hours."
- **Device cookie:** a random device ID to prevent duplicate ratings and enforce limits. No account required.
- **IP address:** hashed (SHA-256) on receipt for anti-abuse; the raw IP is not stored.
- **Page analytics (no cookies, no third party):** page path, referring site (domain only), campaign tags from the link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`), and a coarse device type (mobile or desktop). Clicks on a bar website, bar Instagram, ticket link, or tip link, with the bar name. To estimate unique visitors we keep a hash of the IP address plus the browser string, salted with a value that changes every day; that salt is replaced the next day and is not kept. The raw IP and the full browser string are not stored. Owner magic links and admin pages are not counted. A successful weekend-picks signup is counted as a subscribe event in this same log, without the email address.
- **Weekend picks email:** if someone signs up, we store the email address, the page they signed up from, and campaign tags from the link when those are present. We use the email only to send the weekend picks. We never sell it. Every picks email includes an unsubscribe link. There is no confirmation email before the first send. The raw IP is not stored with the signup. A short-lived in-memory limit slows repeated signup attempts from one connection and is not written down.
**How we use it:** compute Hot Right Now / Week / Streak leaderboards, detect spam and manipulation, operate the service. We don't sell personal data.
**Sponsored placements:** venues may pay for a "Sponsored" card. Sponsors get no access to your data and payment never changes organic scores.
