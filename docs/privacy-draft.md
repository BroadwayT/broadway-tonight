# Broadway Tonight — Privacy Policy (DRAFT — for lawyer review, not legal advice)
_Last updated: [DATE]_
**What we collect**
- **Ratings:** a 1–5 star rating of a *bar*, tagged to the set/band playing at that time. No written reviews or free text.
- **Vibe tags:** optional pick (e.g. loud, chill, packed, dancing, tipsy) attached to a rating.
- **Check-ins:** optional "I'm here" flag (later: approximate location to confirm you're on Broadway; never stored as continuous tracking).
- **Stage photos (optional):** shown publicly for **24 hours only**, then archived (kept in storage, hidden from public). "Photos stay up 24 hours."
- **Device cookie:** a random device ID to prevent duplicate ratings and enforce limits. No account required.
- **IP address:** hashed (SHA-256) on receipt for anti-abuse; the raw IP is not stored.
- **Page analytics (no cookies, no third party):** page path, referring site (domain only), campaign tags from the link (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`), and a coarse device type (mobile or desktop). Clicks on a bar website, bar Instagram, ticket link, or tip link, with the bar name. To estimate unique visitors we keep a hash of the IP address plus the browser string, salted with a value that changes every day; that salt is replaced the next day and is not kept. The raw IP and the full browser string are not stored. Owner magic links and admin pages are not counted.
**How we use it:** compute Hot Right Now / Week / Streak leaderboards, detect spam and manipulation, operate the service. We don't sell personal data.
**Sponsored placements:** venues may pay for a "Sponsored" card. Sponsors get no access to your data and payment never changes organic scores.
