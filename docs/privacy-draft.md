**What we collect**
- **Ratings:** a 1–5 star rating of a *bar*, tagged to the set/band playing at that time. No written reviews or free text.
- **Vibe tags:** optional pick (e.g. loud, chill, packed, dancing, tipsy) attached to a rating.
- **Check-ins:** optional "I'm here" flag (later: approximate location to confirm you're on Broadway; never stored as continuous tracking).
- **Stage photos (optional):** shown publicly for **24 hours only**, then archived (kept in storage, hidden from public). "Photos stay up 24 hours."

- **IP address:** hashed (SHA-256) on receipt for anti-abuse; the raw IP is not stored.
**How we use it:** compute Hot Right Now / Week / Streak leaderboards, detect spam and manipulation, operate the service. We don't sell personal data.
