Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Broadway Tonight is a phone guide to who is playing on Lower Broadway. This page says what we keep, and what we do with it.

Weekend picks email

If you sign up, we store your email address, the page you signed up from, and campaign tags from the link when they are present (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign). We use that email only to send the weekend picks. We never sell it. Every picks email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops the picks. We do not send a confirmation email before the first one.

The signup form does not store your IP address. A short in-memory limit slows repeated tries from the same connection, then that limit is forgotten.

Ratings

  • A 1 to 5 star rating of a bar, tagged to the set that was playing. No written reviews.
  • An optional vibe: loud, chill, packed, dancing, or tipsy.
  • An optional "I'm here" check-in. It is a checkbox, not continuous location tracking.

Device cookie

A random device id, so the same phone cannot rate one set twice and so the hourly limits hold. No account. Clearing site data resets it.

IP address

For ratings, the IP is hashed when it arrives and the raw address is not stored. The hash is used to hide bursts that look like one person rating from many tabs.

Page analytics

No third-party scripts. We record the page path, the referring site's domain, campaign tags, and whether the browser looks like a phone or a desktop. We also count clicks on a bar website, a bar Instagram, a ticket link, or a tip link. To estimate unique visitors we keep a hash of the IP address plus the browser string, salted with a value that changes every day. That salt is replaced the next day and is not kept. The raw IP and the full browser string are not stored. Owner links and the stats page are not counted.

A successful signup also records a subscribe event in that same log. The event does not include your email address.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data. A sponsored card is a paid placement. Sponsors do not get your email or your ratings, and payment does not change scores.
